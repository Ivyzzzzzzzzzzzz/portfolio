Pharmacy Brand: Bringing Joy to Everyday Illnesses

A playful, approachable line of over-the-counter medications to ease the stress of dealing with minor illnesses.

Context

The inspiration for this project comes from a personal experience.

I often had headaches and fevers back then. I’d buy the over-the-counter meds I usually used from local pharmacies.

But their packaging felt cold and lacked emotional warmth. They had plain color schemes, lots of confusing medical jargon, and a detached, impersonal tone.

That’s when I thought about the health supplements I’d used before. These products package made taking supplements feel like a gentle part of daily self-care, not a chore.

If over-the-counter meds are here to help us through small daily discomforts.

Why shouldn’t their packaging feel warm and supportive too?

What Our Brand Can Do

Design Approach

We employ vibrant, bold colors and playful typography to transform the typically sterile OTC category into an engaging, supportive experience that feels warm and approachable.

Emotional Connection

Unlike clinical competitors, our brand uses humor and relatable language to ease the stress of minor ailments, turning a frustrating experience into a moment of comfort.

User-Centric Messaging

By speaking directly to customers (e.g., “Let’s tackle that headache!”), we empower and comfort them, setting us apart from brands that rely solely on clinical efficacy.

Brand Brief
QUIQ

Comes from the phrase “Quick Cure”.

Core Concept

A playful line of over-the-counter medications that uses cheerful colors, lighthearted copy, and warm, friendly typography to ease the stress of dealing with minor illnesses. Product labels are conversational (“I’ve Got a Headache,” “I’m itchy”) to quickly guide customers to the right remedy and add a dash of humor to the experience.

Targeted Symptom Selection

I focused the brand around three common everyday symptom categories: sore throat, body aches, and back pain, to create a concise and approachable OTC product system.

“I’ve Got a Sore Throat”

How It Helps:
Perfect for when your throat feels scratchy or like it’s been overused—Quiq Soothe helps calm the irritation so you can get back to chatting up a storm.

Usage Directions:
Allow one lozenge to dissolve slowly in your mouth every 2–3 hours. The package clearly suggests, “Take a moment for yourself—let every melt-in-your-mouth moment work its magic.”

Packaging Form Exploration

Early packaging form explorations investigating how shape, scale, and opening mechanisms could make over-the-counter medicine feel more approachable and intuitive.

3 Design Concepts
Concept 1:
Speech bubble+friendly

Framing the wordmark within a speech bubble underscores the brand’s conversational tone, like a trusted friend offering advice. It conveys empathy and dialogue , highlighting our commitment to speak directly and supportively to customers.

Concept 2:
Pill in counter

Using overlapped and rounded pill silhouettes to immediately signal the product category while reinforcing playful, approachable aesthetic.

Concept 3:
Initial “Q”

Demand reassurance of product effectiveness and safety, alongside a friendly brand personality—refusing to choose between emotional appeal and reliable symptom relief.

Final

The Quiq design system leans into warmth and conversation: its animated Q-pill motif flexes like a speech bubble, signaling approachable, on-demand support. A modular set of pill shapes, circular accents, and the tail “Q” keeps layouts consistent across all touchpoints, so the brand stays recognizable at any scale.
